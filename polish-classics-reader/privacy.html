Privacy Policy

Polish Classics A1-B1

Effective date: 24 May 2026

Overview

Polish Classics A1-B1 is an offline reading app for Polish learners. The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, microphone data, or user-generated content.

Data Stored On Device

The app stores reading preferences and progress locally on the device, including interface language, selected theme, font settings, last read book, bookmarks, and saved dictionary words. This local data stays on the device and is not sent to an app account or custom backend service.

Offline Content And Dictionary

The reading content and learner dictionary are bundled inside the app. Reading, dictionary lookup, bookmarks, saved words, and reading progress work offline.

Purchases

If the premium book unlock is enabled, purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The app receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock books on that platform. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google and are not available to the app.

Advertising

If ads are enabled for the free books, the app may show Google AdMob interstitial ads only at chapter transitions. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device, advertising, consent, diagnostics, app activity, or ad interaction data according to Google Play services and AdMob policies.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow before requesting ads and provides an advertising privacy options entry point in Settings.

Analytics

The app does not include third-party analytics or a custom backend in this release.

Children

The app is intended for language learning and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or app support, contact learnenglishwithmusicsg@gmail.com.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when app features, analytics, advertising settings, or legal requirements change.
