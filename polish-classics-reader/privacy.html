Privacy Policy

Polish Classics A1-B1

Effective date: 30 August 2026

Overview

Polish Classics A1-B1 is an offline reading app for Polish learners. The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, microphone data, or user-generated content on a server.

Data Stored On Device

The app stores reading preferences and progress locally on the device, including interface language, selected theme, font settings, last read book, bookmarks, saved dictionary words, personal notes attached to saved words, rewarded-ad credit balance, and books unlocked with those credits. This local data is not sent to an app account or custom backend service. Depending on the user's operating-system and device-backup settings, the platform may include local app data in a device or cloud backup.

Offline Content And Dictionary

The reading content and learner dictionary are bundled inside the app. Reading, dictionary lookup, bookmarks, saved words, personal word notes, and reading progress work offline.

Purchases

If the premium book unlock is enabled, purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The app receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock books on that platform. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google and are not available to the app.

Advertising

If ads are enabled for non-premium or legacy-eligible readers, the app may show Google AdMob bottom banners and interstitial ads at eligible chapter transitions. A non-premium reader may also explicitly choose to watch one rewarded ad at a time. A completed rewarded ad adds one device-local credit; 10 credits can be exchanged for access to one selected, ad-supported book on that device. Skipping or dismissing the ad does not add a credit. Credits are not money, cannot be transferred, and are not stored on a developer server. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit.

Before an ad is loaded, the app applies one all-user age-restricted child treatment (ageRestrictedTreatment.child), requests non-personalized ads, and enforces a maximum G content rating. Every user is treated as under the age of consent (TFUA). Under this configuration, Google's User Messaging Platform does not request EEA consent and an advertising privacy-options form is not expected. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or access to the IDFA.

Analytics

The app does not include a separate analytics SDK or a custom backend in this release. Google AdMob may collect advertising and analytics data as described in the Advertising section above.

Children

The app is intended for language learning and does not ask children for personal information. When ads are enabled, the child-directed protections described above apply to every user by default.

Contact

For privacy questions or app support, contact learnenglishwithmusicsg@gmail.com.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when app features, analytics, advertising settings, or legal requirements change.
